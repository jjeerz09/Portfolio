The project

Take an RC car from concept to driving hardware. The team selected the motor and gearing, designed the chassis, steering and drivetrain in CAD, and is now moving into fabrication with a mix of machined and 3D-printed parts. Once it's built, we'll test how it drives and run a finite element analysis of the chassis.

My role

I own the bill of materials, tracking every purchased and fabricated part from design through ordering. I also lead two subsystems: the steering, built around an Ackermann linkage so the inner and outer front wheels turn about the same center, and the rear-wheel assembly.

Where it stands

1 · DesignDone. The full CAD assembly is complete and running.
2 · OrderParts ordered from the BOM. Waiting on delivery.
3 · BuildMachine and print the parts, then assemble the car.
4 · Test & FEADrive testing, plus an FEA study of the chassis.
