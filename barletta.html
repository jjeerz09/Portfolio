PROJECT 1 · MANUFACTURING ENGINEERING

Standardizing production drawings

Prints going to the production floor had no standard format. Extra non-critical detail, inconsistent dimensioning and varying layouts made them harder for manufacturing staff to read. I audited the rail-location and floorplan sheets for every in-scope model, stripped out non-critical detail, normalized how dimensions were shown, and added a bill of materials for part identification.

I then checked the drawings against boats on the production floor, correcting sketches and blocks that didn't match what was actually built. I prioritized the base model of each line, since the many editions are built from those and they set the standard for everything else.

PROJECT 2 · MANUFACTURING ENGINEERING · R&D

Evaluating 3D scanning for as-built floorplans

When a boat comes back for a warranty floor replacement, there's no precise record of how that specific boat was built. The goal was a 3D-scanned, as-built floorplan for every boat, tied to its serial number, to support targeted floor replacements and dealer service.

Working through a five-phase plan, I evaluated scanning approaches against production requirements: accuracy, repeatability, scan time and whether it could keep up at production volume. A handheld scanner was too slow to scale, and phone and tablet apps weren't accurate or repeatable enough, so by the end of the summer the project had moved on to evaluating industrial-grade systems.

PROJECT 3 · DESIGN ENGINEERING

A universal, self-leveling bracket fixture

Each production line had its own bracket fixture setup, which caused alignment inconsistencies, made fixtures awkward and less safe to use, and meant a given pontoon couldn't run down just any line. I developed concepts for a single fixture that levels itself and could support future automation. My manager approved the direction to move toward prototyping. The concepts themselves stay private while the company protects the design.

PROJECT 4 · DESIGN ENGINEERING

3D boat modeling

I also built 3D models of boats to support product visualization and digital documentation, extending the company's digital product records alongside the drawing work.

Takeaways: working with plant managers and engineers on real production problems, scoping projects into phases, and choosing what to prioritize when there isn't time to do everything.
